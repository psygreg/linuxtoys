**Photon Studio** is a free desktop photo editor for macOS, Windows, and Linux. Edit photos, create layered designs, and open or save PSD files. Your work stays on your computer.

- Work with layers and masks, retouch with healing and Liquify, add text and vector shapes, and edit Photoshop documents.
- Photon edits locally. There is no upload step, no queue, and no account needed to open a file and work on it. Even subject selection and background removal run on your own machine.
