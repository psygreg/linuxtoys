Programming is hard. So, instead of doing that, we vibe coded a new Epic Games Launcher that’s way faster and more responsive than the current one, and has a new look.

**Lighter, quicker and connected**

- Get to the fun sooner: faster cold starts, snappier navigation between library and store, and less load on your system while it runs in the background.
- Stay connected: party up and voice chat (Abre em uma nova aba) with friends across Fortnite, Epic Games Store, and the Epic Games app.

**Improved library and download experience**

- Everything about your game in one view: select any game to check its status, activity, details, and play controls on a dedicated page. Connect your PC libraries and see everything you own together within the Launcher.
- Customize your experience: keep collections alongside your library, switch between grid and list views, and customize the top bar with your favorite games and apps.
- Find games faster: search and filter as you type, with a cleaner layout that makes even large libraries easier to navigate. 
- Monitor your downloads: check download, read, and write speeds, as well as active, queued, scheduled and failed downloads in one place.
