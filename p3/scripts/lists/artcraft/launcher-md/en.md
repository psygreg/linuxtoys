One place to install, update and open every app in the **Craft App Suite**: *PhotoCraft, LightCraft, FilmCraft, DesignCraft, VectorCraft, PdfCraft, EffectCraft, WordCraft, DeckCraft, GridCraft, CADCraft* and *SoundCraft* - the right build for your computer, kept up to date.

The Craft apps each ship their own builds for every platform. **ArtCraft Launcher** saves you from hunting through a dozen release pages: it lists every app in the suite, shows which ones you have and which version, installs the right build for your operating system and processor, and tells you when an update is out. It is a small native app that can sit in your menu bar or system tray.

- **One-click installs**: picks the right download for your OS and CPU (DMG, MSI, AppImage, .deb, .rpm and more), checks it against the release's SHA-256 checksums and installs it.
- **Stay up to date**: checks each app's GitHub releases and updates the ones you have. A notification tells you when there's something new. 
- **Always at hand**: opens any Craft app from one window, or from the tray and menu-bar icon. Native egui, no Electron, no web view.
