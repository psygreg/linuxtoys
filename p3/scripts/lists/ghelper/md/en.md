Forget *Armoury Crate* and *Windows*. This unofficial Linux port of **G-Helper** turns your ***ASUS ROG, TUF, Flow, Z13, Ally, Zenbook, Vivobook or ProArt*** into a power user's dream. Fan curves, *AURA* RGB, MUX, battery limits, GPU mode. No bloat. No telemetry. And much more.

- Manages the *ASUS* dGPU (discrete GPU) power state and MUX switch through four modes: **Eco, Standard, Optimized and Ultimate**
- Performance modes: **Silent, Balanced and Turbo**
- Custom fan curves, 8-point drag-to-edit per fan
- Battery charge limit to preserve battery capacity and longevity (40-100%)
- Power limits following CPU PL1/PL2, Dynamic Boost, temperatures
- Screen controls with refresh rate, panel overdrive and MiniLED features
- Keyboard backlight with brightness and RGB (per-key Aura) controls
- Display settings: brightness, gamma, multi-backend
- Undervolting with *AMD Curve Optimizer* or *Intel MSR*
- Audio DSP with EQ, noise suppression, reverb
- ***Anime Matrix*** / Slash LED matrix display control
- ROG Ally controller mode and handheld configuration
- FnLock remapping with custom Fn key bindings

G-Helper also offers **experimental Lenovo support** (IdeaPad / Legion / LOQ / Yoga): performance modes, battery conservation, fan monitoring, PPT limits and keyboard backlight via the mainline ideapad-laptop and lenovo-wmi kernel drivers (kernel 6.17+ for power limits).
